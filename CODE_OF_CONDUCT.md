# Code of Conduct

pitstop follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful, assume good intent, and focus on the work rather than the person.
- Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks aren't accepted anywhere in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other channel.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, report it privately to the maintainer:

- open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/gowtham012/pitstop/security/advisories/new) and mark it as a conduct report; or
- contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously break it, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
